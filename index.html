What I found

01

One phone link dials a fake number.

On every page of your site (28 of 28 in your sitemap), the “Phone: 702-382-0091” line in the contact block shows your real number but is set to call 123-456-7890. Someone on a phone who taps it reaches a dead number. Your header “Call Us” button and the CALL button work correctly.

02

The package pages have typos where people decide.

All six package pages say “Digital Video Donwload NOT incluced”, and the Gift Shop page has “teh”. The page address for your Storybook chapel page also spells “weding”. Small things, but they sit next to the prices.

03

Your business data is thin.

Behind the page, your markup describes Graceland as a generic organization with a name, phone number, logo and social links. It has no street address, no opening hours and no wedding-venue type. Search tools get your address only from the page text and the map.

04

Your vow-renewal page titles don't say “Las Vegas”.

The titles read “Elvis Vow Renewal Packages - Graceland Wedding Chapel” and “Traditional Vow Renewal Packages - Graceland Wedding Chapel”. In my vow-renewal search, you were named seventh of eight.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt only blocks the WordPress admin area. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    You publish an llms.txt file

    /llms.txt exists and lists 28 of your pages with short descriptions. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, and most sites don't have one.

  • Found
    Google listing and socials are linked

    The homepage links to your Google Maps listing and embeds a map for 619 Las Vegas Blvd S. The markup links to your Facebook, Instagram and YouTube.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML holds about 1,950 words of text, including your packages, history and the review counts you list.

  • Gap
    Business data has no address or hours

    The markup type is “Organization” with name, phone, logo and social links. There is no street address, no opening hours, and no wedding-venue or place type.

  • Gap
    A phone link dials a placeholder

    The “Phone: 702-382-0091” line in the contact block on every page links to 123-456-7890. I checked this in the page code and in a desktop browser. How it behaves on a phone is unverified.

  • Note
    Typos on the package pages

    “Donwload” and “incluced” appear on all six package pages. Search tools quote that text.

  • Note
    Vow-renewal titles leave out Las Vegas

    Your two vow-renewal pages are titled “... Packages - Graceland Wedding Chapel”. Other results for the same search put “Las Vegas” in the title.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 15, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
